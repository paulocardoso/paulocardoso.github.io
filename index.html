About me

A clear head.
A wider perspective.

I’m Paulo Cardoso, a CTO focused on problem solving.

Technology gets complicated. I believe the work starts with understanding the challenge, connecting the right ideas, and finding a practical way forward.

How I approach a challenge

Start with the right question.

  1. 01

    Understand first.

    Get close to the problem. Ask what matters, what’s getting in the way, and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. 02

    Connect the pieces.

    Look beyond one tool or one team. Bring technology, people, and context into the same conversation.

  3. 03

    Make the next move.

    Turn understanding into a clear decision. Choose a practical next step, learn from it, and keep moving.
